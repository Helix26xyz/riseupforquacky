Rise Up for Quacky

A duckling died in the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool. We should not look away.

Recent reporting describes a dead duckling floating in the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool after a $14.7 million renovation ran into algae blooms, peeling sealant, and frantic cleanup efforts. The cause of the duckling’s death remains unclear, but the warning is unmistakable: public landmarks should be safe for wildlife, not hazardous to it.

Rise Up for Quacky — because “cause unclear” is not good enough when a vulnerable animal is already dead.

What happened

  • A duckling was found dead in the Reflecting Pool on Sunday.
  • The pool has been plagued by algae, peeling sealant, and emergency treatment efforts.
  • Officials have warned of prosecutions for alleged vandalism while basic questions about wildlife safety remain unanswered.

Why it matters

  • The Reflecting Pool is a national symbol and a shared habitat for birds and other living creatures.
  • High-intensity cleanup chemicals and rushed repairs can have real consequences for animals in the water.
  • Protecting public spaces means demanding stewardship that values life, transparency, and accountability.
